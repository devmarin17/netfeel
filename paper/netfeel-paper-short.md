# NetFeel: Netflix 2046 (short version)

*Short version of [`netfeel-paper.md`](netfeel-paper.md). Full detail, all signals and sources are there.*

## 0. The idea in one line

In 2046 Netflix doesn't ask *what* you want to watch. It asks **how you want to feel.**

## 1. Where Netflix is now

Netflix is strong: $45.18B revenue in 2025. But growth is slowing (Q3 2026: +11.8%, the slowest in about three years), and viewing hours rose only 2% in H1 2026.

The limit is attention. YouTube has 15.8% of US TV time, Netflix 7.7% (Nielsen, Aug 2026). Netflix doesn't have a money problem. It has an attention problem.

Netflix changes its own rules once an experiment proves a new one: never ads became an ad tier, binge-only became live events. That is how it can change again.

## 2. Trends and the five key weak signals

Trends are the tools that already exist: wearables that track heart rate and estimate stress (an estimate, not a measured emotion). Weak signals show where things are going.

## 3. One evening with Noa, 2046

Noa is 29, lives in Amsterdam, they/them. They were 9 in 2026. They wear a Netflix ring.

After a long, bottled-up week, Noa tells their assistant by voice: "I need a good cry tonight." The assistant asks NetFeel. Noa's mood profile stays on their devices, and the assistant only gets the answer.

The TV offers one film and one reason: "Because your breathing slowed and your pulse settled after the hardest scene in X." Noa knows why it was picked.

Halfway through, Noa cries for the first time all week. The film runs to its natural end. No autoplay. NetFeel shows a small feeling receipt: "Heartbroken, then lighter: yes." Noa deletes tonight's reading with one tap.

**What changed by 2046**
- **Watching:** you ask by feeling, often through an assistant. NetFeel picks, then stops.
- **Making:** directors set emotion rails. An opt-in, anonymous scene-level signal shows how scenes really land. Films ship with perspective tracks, and human-made scenes are labelled.
- **Creators:** directors become feeling designers. Actors and creators record perspectives and earn per perspective watched.

## 4. How Netflix stays up to date

The way it always has: heresy, experiment, new rule. The new rule is that *engagement = hours* becomes *engagement = did you get the feeling you came for.*

- **2026:** the signals above.
- **2028:** a 12-week pilot, A/B, bring-your-own wearable or phone camera, two lanes (cozy/sleep, horror/thriller). The metric is State-Hit Rate: the share of evenings where the body confirms the feeling you asked for.
- **2030s:** feeling search in every app, assistants call NetFeel, first perspective tracks, the Netflix wearable bundle.
- **2046:** NetFeel is how most members choose, and State-Hit Rate replaces watch hours.

## 5. NetFeel

A feeling service that works with a wearable you own or one Netflix sells.

1. **Sense.** The wearable reads heart rate, HRV, skin temperature and movement.
2. **Learn.** NetFeel builds your personal mood profile.
3. **Ask.** Search by feeling, in the app or through your assistant.
4. **Own.** Processed on your device, visible, deletable, never sold or used for ads.

**The music logic:** people pick songs by how they feel, not by genre or BPM. NetFeel does that for film.

**Showcase: perspectives.** Selected films let you switch whose eyes you watch through. The director marks the doorways. The story stays the same, the feeling changes. It builds empathy and gives creators a new format.

**What works today (MVP).** The spine demo takes a real Garmin heart rate into a taste model the member owns, and Claude Desktop asks it for a pick. The Reaction Lab reads a camera reaction on the device.

## 6. Consequences

**Top wins:** viewers who stay in control; creators with a new format and real feedback; Netflix with a new metric and new trust.

**Top harms:**
- **A sensor divide.** People without wearables get a worse Netflix. Answer: phone camera and plain feeling search work without one.
- **Feeling data used for ads.** If the rules slip, it becomes an ad-targeting goldmine.

**Guardrails:** the Skip button (consent per moment); every pick says why; owned by the member; never nudging (the session ends when you reach the feeling); human rails (directors set the range, AI works inside it and is labelled).

## Close
