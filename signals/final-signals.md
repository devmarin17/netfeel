# Final weak signals to act on

The nine signals selected from the paper's longlist (see [`../paper/netfeel-paper.md`](../paper/netfeel-paper.md) §2), reviewed one at a time.
These are the inputs for the consequence visuals: the [2046 world wheels](netfeel-world-wheels.html) and the [nine-signal overview](netfeel-consequence-overview.html).

**Core line:** in 2046 Netflix doesn't ask what you want to watch. It asks how you want to feel. **Sense → Learn → Ask → Own.**
**Stance:** human-to-human connection, human-made content, long-form culture, no AI slop. Quality is how the viewer feels, not budget.

## A. Weak signals to map (9)

| # | Signal | Built from | What it means for us (author's framing) | NetFeel step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Media used to reach a feeling** | W6 sleep/ASMR + W1 comfort rewatching | Two things: people choose by feeling instead of genre, *and* they use media as a tool to reach a state ("calm by 23:00"). Rewatching comfort shows is the same behaviour. Netflix sells states, not hours. | Learn, Ask |
| 2 | **The assistant becomes (almost) the remote** | W18 | Your personal AI assistant asks NetFeel for you and replaces most of the browsing. The Netflix app still exists; NetFeel is an extension that works both in the app and through the assistant. | Ask |
| 3 | **Trusting machines with feelings, but trust wins over usage** | W10 + W12 | Kids already share feelings with AI, so emotion-aware media will feel normal in 2046. They use AI but don't love it, so the winner is whoever earns trust and enjoyment, not whoever gets the most use. | Own |
| 4 | **Mood search has already been tested** | W9 | Netflix tried mood-based AI search in 2025. The tech is ready now, so NetFeel pushes it much further. | Ask |
| 5 | **AI slop floods the feeds** | W2 | Netflix answers in two ways: a no-slop content policy *and* a visible "verified human-made" badge. | Trust |
| 6 | **Counter-trend: back to curated, human-made** | T5 + author's note | Discovery moved to social today, but future generations leave social and short-form for well-curated, human-made content that gives them the feeling they want right now. | Ask, Trust |
| 7 | **Under-16 social media bans** | W7 | NetFeel is the safe alternative regulators and parents accept: curated, human-edited picks for kids, plus parental controls over what gets recommended. | Trust, Own |
| 8 | **A human-made making-of for every title** | W15 + author's note | A behind-the-scenes piece for every film proves it's human-made and keeps fans in the world between releases. | Trust |
| 9 | **Rules for emotion data** | W19 EU AI Act + W20 DMA portability | Emotion recognition is high-risk (consent, transparency, user control), and you can take your data with you. That is the evidence for "your mood profile is yours". | Own |

## B. Enablers (in the story, not mapped)

Proof that NetFeel is easier to launch, because the tech already exists:
- **T1** wearables became continuous health monitors
- **T3** machines already read stress (WHOOP, Oura)
- **W4** media that reacts to your heartbeat (moved here from the weak signals)
- **T2** screens fill young people's days (the time NetFeel moves back to the TV)

## C. Contrast (story only, not mapped)

- **W16 Microdramas:** shows what we're not. We protect long-form, human-made content and focus on feeling, not clickbait.
