    What the four wheels share

    • Trust becomes scarce. Gatekeeping assistants, emotion-data leaks and AI slop all make people ask who they can trust.
    • Choice moves to machines. Assistants and mood-picking mean fewer conscious choices and narrower tastes.
    • Human-made gains value. The more AI fills screens and conversations, the more real human work and connection are worth.
    • Regulation follows everywhere. Neutrality rules, protected emotion data, wellbeing claims, AI labels.
