NetFeel weak signals

Each signal is named as what we can already see today. The line under it explains it in plain words.

Must-haves

1. Everyone is getting a personal AI assistant, and it's starting to act for them

Economics · W18

About 800 million people use ChatGPT every week, and assistants have started to buy and book things on our behalf. Next, they'll choose what we watch. “I need to calm down” goes to the assistant, and the assistant asks Netflix.

2. Kids already share their feelings with AI, but they don't love it

Social relationships · W10 + W12

72% of US teens have used an AI companion, but only 11% enjoy AI “a lot”. Sharing feelings with machines is becoming normal, and people will stay only with the ones they trust.

3. People already use media to reach a feeling, not to pick a genre

Individual behaviour · W6 + W1

Sleep sounds, ASMR and endless rewatches of Friends and The Office show it: people already choose content for how it makes them feel. Most ASMR viewers (about 63%) use it to fall asleep.

4. AI slop is flooding the feeds

Education & culture · W2

More than 20% of the videos YouTube shows new users are AI slop, and so are 57% of the videos in TikTok's kids category. The more slop there is, the more “verified human-made” is worth.

More signals, by category

Individual behaviour

5. Netflix has already tested searching by mood

W9

In 2025 Netflix let some users search by feeling in an AI beta. The idea is proven; NetFeel is the version that works.

6. Young people may turn away from endless feeds

T5 · our counter-trend

Today 73% of Gen Z discover content on social media. Our bet is that the next generation gets tired of feeds and wants fewer, curated, human-made things.

Politics & government

7. Laws are making emotion data the user's own

W19 + W20

The EU AI Act treats emotion recognition as high-risk, and the Digital Markets Act lets you take your data with you. By law, your feelings data is becoming yours.

8. Countries are banning social media for under-16s

W7

Australia's ban has been law since December 2025, yet 81.5% of kids still use the banned apps. A ban alone doesn't work, so families need a safe place kids actually want to be.

Education & culture

9. Fans want more than the show itself

W15

46% of fans look for creator content around what they watch. A human-made making-of for every title keeps them in the story's world and proves real people made it.

Environment

No signal. W21 (AI data centres use more and more power) was dropped. It's the first candidate to bring back if the map needs all six categories.

Enablers

The tech already exists. Not weak signals.

  • Wearables became continuous health monitors (T1): watches and rings track heart rate, skin temperature and sleep all day.
  • Machines already read stress (T3): WHOOP and Oura sell stress scores based on heart-rate variability.
  • Media already reacts to your heartbeat (W4): a horror game that changes its music and enemies to match the player's pulse.
  • Screens fill young people's days (T2): US teens spend over 8 hours a day on screen entertainment.

Contrast

  • Microdramas (W16): short emotional hits made for phones. NetFeel is the opposite: long-form, human-made, feeling over clickbait.
