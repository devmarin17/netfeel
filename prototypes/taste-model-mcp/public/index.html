          Limits, said out loud

          What 2026 can't tell

          Heart rate is arousal, not emotion

          A watch separates calm from keyed-up. It cannot tell dread from hype from tears: for those three, the receipt only checks that your heart went up.

          "Stress" means above your own resting rate

          Coffee, stairs and stage fright count too. The model uses one number, your bpm over your resting rate, and shows it to you.

          The history is invented

          Daan's 90 evenings are simulated. The pilot in our roadmap (2028) is the step that replaces them with real, consented ones.

          The counts overlap

          A nature show carries five tags, so "nature 16/16" re-counts the same evenings. Evidence is thin, which is why the model shows its counts, not just a score.

          What 2046 could add (concept)

          Ring HRV and skin temperature, breathing, gaze on the TV, your calendar. Each one only with your consent, per moment: the same Skip button.

          No watch? Words still work

          Without a heart read the model uses what you tell it and your history only. The body is an optional second opinion, never a requirement.
