# Threat Map — Who Is Fighting Netflix for Humanity's Attention Hours

**Sources:** Nielsen, earnings releases, trade press and platform newsrooms (data from mid-2025 onward).
**Core idea:** engagement time is **zero-sum**. Total media time is capped at ~the waking day (**12h 42m/day with media in 2025 — up only 2 minutes**, OMD). Every front below is a fight over slices of that pie.

## 2. Front-by-front

### FRONT 1 — YouTube (the #1 threat)
**The facts:** Nielsen's Gauge: YouTube 12.7% of US TV time (Jan 2026) → 13.4% (Apr) → record **14.2% (Jul 2026)** — 6+ straight months as the single biggest "distributor" on American TVs. **97.3%** of US under-12 viewers use YouTube; 1 in 5 Gen Alpha kids name it their *first* TV stop (Ofcom, Jul 2025); 78% of US kids 2–12 use it vs 72% VOD (Precise TV, Sept 2025). The flywheel: **$100B paid to creators 2021–24**; YouTube revenue **>$60B for 2025** (ads $41B + subs ~$19–20B) — Netflix's ~$17–18B content budget buys ~1,500 titles; YouTube's payout machine funds *millions* of shows. Plus: **#1 podcast platform in the US** (Edison, 2025–26). The erosion goes both ways: MrBeast's *Beast Games* hit 50M viewers on Prime — creators graduate *into* premium TV. What YouTube still lacks: serialized prestige drama with a single vision — Netflix's remaining moat, under siege.
Sources: nielsen.com (Jan 20, 2026) · emarketer.com (Gen Alpha) · tubefilter.com (Jul 30, 2025) · variety.com (Feb 2026, YouTube revenue) · edisonresearch.com (Infinite Dial 2025) · businessinsider.com (Jan 2025)

**What this means for Netflix 2046**
- The habit-formation war is already lost for the *next* generation — kids' default screen is YouTube. A 2046 concept must give the Netflix of 2046 a reason a 10-year-old in 2030 opens *it* first.
- Netflix's durable edge is *finished, financed, serialized storyworlds* (Stranger Things = 15B minutes in one month) — weaponize events + story IP; don't try to out-creator YouTube.
- If YouTube is the infinite feed of everything, Netflix 2046 must be the opposite pole: the trusted place where stories *end* and *matter* (the anti-algorithm brand) — or own a social layer YouTube can't replicate.

### FRONT 2 — TikTok (the rescued cultural operating system)
**The facts:** ban saga resolved **Jan 2026**: US JV — 50% Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX, ByteDance keeps 19.9%. Usage: **~1.9B MAU**, ~95 min/day global average, US adults ~52 min/day, teens ~90 min/day. **49% of US consumers have used TikTok as a search engine** (Adobe, Feb 2026; ~64% of Gen Z) — TikTok, not Netflix, increasingly decides "what should I watch tonight." Uploads now reach **60 minutes**; first TikTok Awards US (Dec 18, 2025) streamed on TikTok *and Tubi* — straight out of the linear-TV playbook. Add LIVE commerce and TikTok Shop: an entertainment network with a shopping mall attached.
Sources: reuters.com (Jan 23, 2026) · sqmagazine.co.uk · adobe.com/express (Feb 2026) · newsroom.tiktok.com (Awards)

**What this means for Netflix 2046**
- TikTok owns *demand creation*; Netflix owns *fulfillment* (the 2-hour session). A 2046 concept must collapse that stack — or formalize the pipeline (TikTok-native story formats that resolve into Netflix storyworlds).
- The durable threat is TikTok as Gen Z's *search + social proof* layer: 2046 marketing must be born clip-able.
- 60-minute uploads + Tubi partnerships hint TikTok could bolt on free ad-supported long-form — worst case for Netflix's younger, ad-tier cohorts.

### FRONT 3 — Games-as-media platforms (Roblox / Fortnite / Minecraft)
**The facts:** Roblox Q3 2025: **151.5M DAU (+70%), 39.6B quarterly hours (+91%)**, creators earned **>$1.5B in 2025** (Roblox DevEx) — Hollywood-grade payouts for an audience averaging 11–16. And it's **coming to smart TVs (2026)**: UGC worlds on the living-room screen next to the TV apps. Fortnite x Simpsons (Nov 2025): a month-long TV season *inside a game*, **2.6M concurrents** peak; Disney's **$1.5B** Epic stake (universe slipped to Fall 2026). *A Minecraft Movie*: **$960M worldwide** on a $150M budget — game IP → box office → YouTube is now the most reliable hit pipeline in entertainment. Generational inversion: **39% of Gen Alpha teens spend 3+ hrs/day gaming vs ~30% watching TV** (Attest, 2025). Netflix itself unleashed Squid Game/Stranger Things into Roblox and Fortnite (~78M visits in 10 days for one promo) — using these worlds as marketing, i.e., *ceding* the hangout layer Disney gets to build.

> **⚠️ UPDATE (Oct 2026):** the 151.5M DAU figure above was a **spike, not a base rate** — Roblox's Q2 2026 shareholder letter reports **123M DAU (+10% YoY)** after two quarters of decline, while monetization keeps growing (revenue +36% YoY) and RDC 2026 announced experiences going **standalone-app** (⚠️ single-aggregator sourcing). Re-weight accordingly: Roblox = **plateauing engagement economy**; YouTube = **still-compounding generational default**. Full evidence: [`sources/source-library.md`](../sources/source-library.md), Conflict 1.
Sources: sec.gov (Roblox Q3'25 letter, Oct 30, 2025) · about.roblox.com · thisweekinvideogames.com · boxofficemojo.com · advanced-television.com (Oct 13, 2025) · variety.com (2025)

**What this means for Netflix 2046**
- "Watching" is becoming a *mode inside* worlds (concerts in Fortnite, premieres in Roblox, seasons as game events). A 2046 Netflix concept must answer where *its* world is — not just its catalog.
- Netflix's Roblox/Fortnite presence is marketing, not territory. A 2046 Netflix could be a persistent "storyverse."
- The 2046 core audience (born ~2020–2035) will have logged more Roblox hours than TV hours; interactive-adjacent storytelling is table stakes.

### FRONT 4 — Streamer-vs-streamer (the incumbents regroup)
**The facts:** **Disney+** = the everything-app (132M subs; Hulu folded in through 2026; ESPN DTC $29.99; streaming finally profitable). **Amazon** = sports+ads flywheel (TNF 15.33M avg, +60%; NBA added; Beast Games 50M/25 days; Prime ads default since 2024 — the largest *new* TV ad inventory pool of the decade; Netflix competes with Amazon's *P&L patience*). **Paramount Skydance closed the $110.9B WBD acquisition (Oct 6, 2026)**; **HBO Max + Paramount+ will combine**, framed around AI efficiency — one rival with WB library + HBO prestige + CBS + sports + Skydance tech money. **Apple TV**: F1 US rights ($750M/5yr), NBCU bundle, all-time-high viewership — doesn't need streaming P&L to work. **JioHotstar: 300M subs (Jul 2025)**, free IPL, 237M digital viewers for one final — the next billion subscribers are won with free/cheap, local, mobile-first models. Global streaming subscription revenue: **$157.1B in 2025, ~$171B projected 2026** — growth now almost entirely outside the US.
Sources: thewaltdisneycompany.com · sportsbusinessjournal.com (Sep 25, 2026) · en.wikipedia.org (WBD acquisition) · deadline.com (Oct 2026) · hollywoodreporter.com (Oct 2025) · economictimes.indiatimes.com (Jul 2025)

**What this means for Netflix 2046**
- The market splits into *super-bundles* (Disney/ESPN, Paramount+HBO Max, Amazon) vs Netflix alone; a 2046 concept must say what Netflix bundles — story + play + live moments + creator IP — that no super-bundle replicates.
- Live sports is the bundling glue everywhere except Netflix; a 2046 Netflix likely needs its own "always-on spine" (sports, live events, or something that functions like sports).
- JioHotstar's 300M says the global map is multi-polar — assume free/cheap, local, mobile-first wins the next billion.

### FRONT 5 — AI-native entertainment (the wild card that already died once)
**The facts:** the **Sora arc**: 627K iOS downloads in week 1 (ChatGPT-launch pace) → likeness/copyright revolt (MLK estate block; Cranston/SAG-AFTRA guardrails; MPAA demands) → installs **−32% (Dec 2025), −45% (Jan 2026)** → **shut down April 26, 2026**; Disney's **$1B** Sora-in-Disney+ deal died with it. Reading: infinite synthetic video is *demand-real* but *feed-hostile* — novelty without narrative or trust doesn't retain. Meanwhile **AI slop is already in the pipes: >20% of videos shown to new YouTube users are "AI slop"** (Kapwing via Guardian, Dec 27, 2025); leading slop channels earn ~$117M/yr. And **Character.AI: ~45M MAU averaging ~2 hrs/day — Netflix-level engagement from relationships rather than shows** (Google paid $2.7B to license; teen-harm lawsuits settled from Jan 2026). Hollywood's own line: Lionsgate-Runway struggled; Netflix shipped the first GenAI *final footage* (El Eternauta, Jul 2025); OpenAI's *Critterz* (AI feature, <$30M) is the first real stress test.
Sources: variety.com (Mar 24, 2026) · theguardian.com (Dec 27, 2025; Jul 18, 2025) · businessofapps.com · cnbc.com (Jan 7, 2026) · wsj.com (Critterz)

**What this means for Netflix 2046**
- Sora's death is the most useful datapoint in this file: raw generation doesn't beat *authored worlds* — but by 2046 generation cost → 0, so Netflix's moat must move from producing content to owning **story IP, consented likenesses, and audience trust**.
- AI companions already match Netflix's per-user engagement (~2h/day). Treat *relationship* (characters who know you) as the competitor's playbook — and build the ethically defensible version first.
- Netflix already crossed the GenAI threshold with a trust-forward framing; a 2046 Netflix could make "human-meaningful in an infinite-content world" the explicit brand promise — the anti-slop position YouTube currently cannot take (20%+ of its new-user feed is slop).

### FRONT 6 — Music / audio crossover (the hours video never sees)
**The facts:** Spotify **761M MAU (Q1 2026)**; video podcasts: **500K+ shows, watch time doubled YoY**; video podcasts get ~47% higher engagement than audio-only. Audiobooks: **listening hours +60% in 2025**; half of all audiobook listeners joined *in 2025*; Audiobooks+ passed **1M subs / $100M ARR**, expanding to 180+ markets. Why it eats video time: it's **screen-optional** — any hour Netflix can't serve (driving, running, working) is monetized by audio without competition. And it's converging: 48% of Americans 12+ have both listened to and watched a podcast (Edison 2025).
Sources: newsroom.spotify.com (2024–2025) · selfpublishingadvice.org · musicbusinessworldwide.com · podnews.net (Infinite Dial 2025)

**What this means for Netflix 2046**
- The addressable "attention day" is bigger than the screen day: a 2046 Netflix that only exists on screens forfeits ~30% of waking hours; audio-native story formats are white space.
- Spotify's video podcasts prove the same content wins across both attention contexts — **format fluidity (watch/listen/play) should be a 2046 design principle, not a feature.**

### FRONT 7 — The attention math (deltas and the zero-sum tension)
**The facts:** May 2025: streaming (44.8%) > broadcast+cable (44.2%) for the first time ever. Dec 2025: streaming record **47.5%**; Dec 13, 2025 = first day streaming **>50%** of TV ever (50.4%); Christmas Day: **55.1B streaming minutes, 54% of all TV**. May 2026: 48.6%. Within streaming: YouTube wins the **baseline** (14.2%), Netflix wins **event spikes** (9.0% record → ~8%), Prime rises with sports (4.3%), broadcast+cable shrinks toward ~41.6% — and that dying pool is the #1 source of *new* streaming hours; the harvest is ending. Total media time is NOT growing: **12h 42m/day (2025), up only 2 minutes** (OMD). Generational inversion: Gen Z spends **54% more time on social platforms** than average; 58% spend more time on social video than streaming; creator content now outranks studio content as "most personally resonant" (Deloitte 2025). Who takes hours from whom: YouTube takes linear's hours and Netflix's *habit* time; TikTok takes search and under-30 lean-back minutes; Roblox/Fortnite take under-16s (Netflix nearly absent from their self-reported day); super-bundles merely consolidate each other; Netflix takes from linear TV — steady, profitable, **no longer the frontier**.
Sources: nielsen.com (Jun 17, 2025; Jan 20, 2026; Jul 28, 2026) · omd.fi (2025) · deloitte.com (Digital Media Trends 2025) · theglobalstatistics.com (GWI)

**What this means for Netflix 2046**
- Netflix is the *profit leader* of a category (SVOD) that is no longer the *growth frontier* of attention. The 2046 concept must name which frontier Netflix conquers next (interactive worlds, creator IP, live events, AI-native story) — or accept managed decline by 2046.
- Zero-sum math: Netflix's ~9% is trapped between YouTube's ~14% baseline and the super-bundles' consolidation. A winning play either creates a *new measuring stick* (a third screen category) or buys into the baseline (creators, sports-like live, kids' worlds).
- The unit of competition is no longer the "show" — it's the **daily habit** (feed, world, companion, playlist). Netflix's habit (2 hrs/day/member) is the only one on this list with no social, no UGC, no interactivity.

## 3. Top 5 existential threats to Netflix by 2046 — ranked

### 1. YouTube
- **Killer stat:** **14.2% of US TV time (Jul 2026 record)** vs Netflix ~8%; **97.3%** of under-12s on platform; **$100B** paid to creators.
- **Why existential:** the audience Netflix needs in 2046 is forming its taste on YouTube *today*. YouTube already owns the living room, the podcast layer, and the default habit of the next two generations.
- **Netflix's required counter-move:** don't out-creator YouTube — out-*finish* it. Double down on finished, financed storyworlds + live event supremacy, and build (or buy) the creator pipeline Beast Games proved can graduate into premium TV.

### 2. The games-as-media complex (Roblox + Epic/Fortnite, Disney inside)
- **Killer stat:** Roblox **39.6B hours in ONE quarter (+91%)**; Gen Alpha: **39% game 3+ hrs/day vs 30% TV**.
- **Why existential:** if watching collapses into playing, Netflix's core format is the thing being displaced — for the exact cohort that will be 2046's core subscribers. And Roblox is moving onto the TV screen itself in 2026.
- **Netflix's required counter-move:** a persistent Netflix *world*, not a games tab — licensed storyverses with creator revenue share (UEFN-style economics), playable episodes riding its cloud-to-TV stack.

### 3. The Paramount Skydance–WBD super-bundle (+ Disney/ESPN, Amazon sports)
- **Killer stat:** **$110.9B merger closed Oct 6, 2026** → HBO Max + Paramount+ merging inside a ~$70B-revenue giant; flanked by Disney's everything-app and Amazon's TNF/NBA/ads flywheel.
- **Why existential:** consolidation turns Netflix's solo scale into a niche and hands "the default general bundle" — with sports as the glue Netflix refuses to buy — to someone else.
- **Netflix's required counter-move:** an always-on live spine (regional exclusive events at WBC-Japan scale, weekly rather than seasonal), plus a bundle of its own: story + play + live + creator IP that no super-bundle replicates.

### 4. TikTok (post-ban, Americanized)
- **Killer stat:** **49% of US consumers use it as a search engine**; 95 min/day global; ban threat removed Jan 2026.
- **Why existential:** TikTok decides what culture wants to watch; Netflix becomes the fulfillment layer. If TikTok bolts on free ad-supported long-form (60-min uploads + Tubi partnerships hint at it), it attacks Netflix's younger ad-tier cohorts directly.
- **Netflix's required counter-move:** born-clip-able storytelling — every 2046 title ships with its own demand-creation surface, and the vertical feed (launched Apr 2026) becomes a story format, not just a discovery skin.

### 5. AI-native entertainment (the post-Sora generation: Meta Vibes, Character.AI, infinite synthetic video)
- **Killer stat:** Character.AI users average **~2 hrs/day — Netflix-level engagement**; **>20%** of videos shown to new YouTube users are AI slop; Sora hit 1M downloads faster than ChatGPT, then died in 7 months.
- **Why existential:** when content is infinite and free, attention migrates to (a) infinite personalized feeds and (b) relationships that love you back. Sora proved the demand is real; its death proves novelty without trust doesn't retain — the scarce assets become story IP, consented likeness, and human meaning.
- **Netflix's required counter-move:** pre-emptively claim the trust layer — "authored, human-meaningful, consent-first" as the explicit brand promise, licensed characters that talk back with compliance plumbing (EU AI Act–style labels), and the ethical version of companionship before anyone else builds it.

*(Bubbling under: Spotify — quietly converting screenless hours into video hours; Apple TV — patient money + F1 + bundles; AI companions as the first true post-TV entertainment category.)*
