## 1. What Netflix is

- American media/entertainment company, founded **August 29, 1997** by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph in Scotts Valley, CA. HQ: Los Gatos, California. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix,_Inc.
- Flagship product: the Netflix streaming service — subscription video-on-demand (SVOD), original + acquired films/TV. **Most-subscribed VOD service in the world: ~325M paid memberships in 190+ countries** (as of Jan 20, 2026, Wikipedia's figure; Netflix stopped official reporting in Q1 2025 — last official count: 301.6M, end of 2024). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix
- Self-description: *"We are here to entertain the world, one fan at a time"*; "entertaining over half a billion people in more than 190 countries and 50 languages." Source: https://about.netflix.com/en
- **Total audience incl. extra members: 700M+ people** (company estimate, Jan 2025). Source: https://deadline.com/2025/01/netflix-q4-2024-earnings-subscribers-nfl-squid-game-1236262728/
- Available everywhere except China, Russia, Crimea, North Korea, Syria. Interface in 50+ languages. 22nd most-visited website in the world (2026). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix
- Leadership: **co-CEOs Ted Sarandos and Greg Peters** (since Jan 2023 — https://about.netflix.com/news/ted-sarandos-greg-peters-co-ceos-netflix). Founder Reed Hastings was Executive Chairman from 2023 and **left the board in June 2026**; board chair is now Jay Hoag. Sources: https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/netflix-ceos-praise-reed-hastings-selfless-disciplined-graceful-1236724101/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix,_Inc.
- Self-framing on market position: **less than 10% of TV time in every market** (Jan 2025 shareholder letter); CFO put global TV viewing share at ~5% (July 2026) — headroom, not saturation. Sources: https://deadline.com/2025/01/netflix-q4-2024-earnings-subscribers-nfl-squid-game-1236262728/ ; https://defenseworld.net/2026/07/18/netflix-q2-earnings-call-highlights.html
- M&A stance: **"primarily builders, not buyers"** (CFO Spencer Neumann, July 2026) — see the Warner Bros. saga, §7. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/16/netflix-nflx-earnings-q2-2026.html

## 2. Business model

### 2.1 Core: subscription streaming
- **~95%+ of revenue is recurring monthly subscriptions** (mix varies by market). No annual plans — monthly billing only. Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/24926
- US tiers after the **March 26, 2026** increase (+$1–2 on all):

| Plan | Price (US, Mar 2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard with ads | **$8.99/mo** | was $7.99 |
| Standard | **$19.99/mo** | was $17.99 |
| Premium (4K) | **$26.99/mo** | was $24.99 |
| Extra member add-on | $6.99 (ads) / $9.99 (ad-free) | outside-household users, from the 2023 password crackdown |

  Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/26/netflix-raises-prices-across-all-streaming-plans.html
- Price hikes churn "consistent with prior changes" — Netflix has the lowest churn of any streamer. Source: https://www.thecurrent.com/media/streaming-no-playbook-inside-netflix-quest-double-ad-revenue-3-billion
- The 2022–2024 monetization levers: **paid sharing** (free password sharing ended 2023 → paid "extra member" accounts) + the **ad tier**. Source: https://deadline.com/2025/01/netflix-q4-2024-earnings-subscribers-nfl-squid-game-1236262728/
- A **free tier** "could make sense in some markets" but has "no near-term plans" (Greg Peters, July 2026) — cannibalization risk. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/16/netflix-nflx-earnings-q2-2026.html

### 2.2 Advertising — the second act

*(Full experiment ledger: [experiments.md §2.6](experiments.md#26-the-ad-supported-tier-nov-2022--2026--from-never-ever-to-250m-viewers).)*
- Ad tier launched **Nov 3, 2022** at $6.99/mo US. Audience: 40M MAU (May 2024) → 70M (Nov 2024) → 94M (May 2025) → **190M monthly active viewers** (Nov 2025, new co-viewing-based metric) → **~250M viewers by May 2026** (Forbes). Sources: https://adwave.com/resources/netflix-ad-tier-subscribers ; https://deadline.com/2025/11/netflix-ads-1236606573/ ; https://forbes.com/sites/andymeek/2026/05/16/if-netflixs-ad-tier-audience-was-a-country-it-would-rank-among-the-largest-on-earth
- Ad revenue: **~$1.5B in 2025 (~2.5× 2024 — sources describe the same growth as "+250%" or "a 150% increase") → ~$3B targeted 2026 → ~$8B projected 2030** (Omdia, via WARC). 2025 was the best ad-sales quarter ever in Q3; US upfront commitments doubled. Sources: https://www.thecurrent.com/media/streaming-no-playbook-inside-netflix-quest-double-ad-revenue-3-billion ; https://variety.com/2025/tv/news/netflix-subscribers-earnings-q3-2025-1236557877/
- Deliberately light ad load: **~4–5 min/hour** (vs 15–20 on linear TV); CPMs $25–65; 12 markets ≈ 65% of global ad spend; in-house ad tech (US Apr 2025, all markets Jun 2025); ~80% of CTV buys programmatic. Source: https://www.thecurrent.com/media/streaming-no-playbook-inside-netflix-quest-double-ad-revenue-3-billion
- 45% of US Netflix households watch on the ad tier (Comscore, Aug 2025; up from 34% in 2024); 55%+ of new sign-ups in ad markets. Source: https://adwave.com/resources/netflix-ad-tier-subscribers

### 2.3 Beyond subscriptions: the portfolio (2024–2026)
Live sports, games, physical venues, podcasts, merch, theatrical — all bundled inside one membership (details in §4). First-ever linear carriage deal: Netflix France carrying TF1's live channels + on-demand (announced mid-2025, live ~2026). Source: https://www.businessinsider.com/netflix-video-podcast-push-shows-to-challenge-youtube-2025-11

## 5. Content strategy

- **Originals + licensed + franchise.** Originals were half the US library by 2022 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix). Strategy: own the hits, license the library, then franchise winners across games, experiences, merch, spinoffs, podcasts. Co-productions matter (KPop Demon Hunters was made with Sony Pictures Animation — https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/kpop-demon-hunters-most-popular-netflix-film).
- **Local-language originals = the core differentiator.** **2025 was the first year a majority (52%) of Netflix original TV releases were non-English** (Ampere Analysis; Spanish #1 at 21% of new original seasons; Korea grew fastest). Sources: https://www.ampereanalysis.com/insight/a-first-for-netflix-non-english-content-takes-the-lead-in-tv-originals ; https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/squid-game-korean-content-surge-netflix-non-english-record-1236665198/
- Commissioning in ~20 languages; owned studios: Albuquerque, Shepperton (London), Tres Cantos (Madrid), Martini (Vancouver), Pinewood/Cinespace (Toronto); Korea is the biggest non-English hub. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix,_Inc.
- Non-English titles = **over a third of all viewing**. 2H 2025 standouts: Squid Game S3 (79M views), Caramelo Brazil (53M), Troll 2 Norway (44M), Ángela Spain (36M), French Lover France (33M). Source: https://about.netflix.com/news/what-we-watched-the-second-half-of-2025
- **Franchise flywheels** (hits become universes):
  - *Squid Game* — most-watched title ever (S1: 265.2M views); reality show, games, Netflix House trials. Source: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/top10/most-popular/tv-non-english
  - *Wednesday* — S2 #1 of 2H 2025 (124M views); S1 most-watched English season ever (252.1M). Source: https://about.netflix.com/news/what-we-watched-the-second-half-of-2025
  - *KPop Demon Hunters* — **most-watched Netflix film ever** (236M views by Aug 2025; 481.6M in 2H 2025 alone); its theatrical sing-along topped the US box office; Mattel + Hasbro merch; sequel greenlit. Sources: https://www.forbes.com/sites/maryroeloffs/2025/08/26/kpop-demon-hunters-breaks-record-as-netflixs-most-watched-movie-ever ; https://variety.com/2025/tv/news/netflix-subscribers-earnings-q3-2025-1236557877/
  - *Stranger Things* — 5 seasons 2016–2025 (S5: 94M views in 2H 2025); stage play, animation, games, House experiences. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things_(franchise)
  - Also: Bridgerton (brand-partnership machine — https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2026/03/04/bridgerton-brand-partnerships-strategy), Knives Out, Extraction, Money Heist, One Piece, The Witcher, Happy Gilmore 2 (135M views).
- **Films, animation, kids, docs:** anime >4% of all viewing (2H 2025); kids/family a fortress category (Gabby's Dollhouse 108M views across seasons; Ms. Rachel 73M; Sesame Street deal 2025); documentaries a pillar (Unknown Number 57M). Source: https://about.netflix.com/news/what-we-watched-the-second-half-of-2025
- **The metrics Netflix uses:** "views" = hours viewed ÷ runtime (91-day windows for all-time lists); semiannual Engagement Reports → annual from 2027. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/16/netflix-nflx-earnings-q2-2026.html

## 7. The Warner Bros. saga (Dec 2025 – Apr 2026): who Netflix almost became

- **Dec 5, 2025** — Netflix signs agreement to acquire Warner Bros. (studios, HBO, HBO Max, WB Games) for **$82.7B enterprise value / $72B equity** ($27.75/share), post-spinoff of the cable networks. $2–3B projected synergies; $5.8B breakup fee; antitrust opposition. Sarandos: "Our mission has always been to entertain the world." Sources: https://variety.com/2025/tv/news/netflix-to-acquire-warner-bros-82-7-billion-deal-1236601034/ ; https://about.netflix.com/news/netflix-to-acquire-warner-bros
- **Feb 26, 2026** — WBD board deems Paramount Skydance's higher bid "superior"; **Netflix declines to match and withdraws**. Sources: https://www.latimes.com/politics/story/2026-02-26/warner-bros-discovery-shifts-gears-says-it-now-favors-paramount-deal-over-netflix ; https://www.foxbusiness.com/media/netflix-backs-out-warner-bros-bidding-war-after-paramount-made-supperior-offer
- **Apr 23, 2026** — WBD shareholders approve Paramount's $31/share offer (with Netflix's breakup fee paid). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acquisition_of_Warner_Bros._Discovery_by_Paramount_Skydance
- **Why it matters for a 2046 concept:** it revealed (a) Netflix's appetite to become a century-scale studio conglomerate, (b) its antitrust/political constraints ("unequaled market power" — Rep. Issa), and (c) its willingness to walk away over price/regulatory risk — see the experiment-pattern "there is a walk-away price" in [experiments.md](experiments.md).

## 8. Strategic themes (synthesis for the 2046 prompt)

1. **One product → a portfolio:** subscription video + ads + live + games + podcasts + venues + merch + theatrical, cross-sold inside a single membership.
2. **Growth re-defined:** subscribers are no longer the north star (reporting stopped 2025); revenue, operating margin (~29% in 2025), and engagement hours are. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/16/netflix-nflx-earnings-q2-2026.html
3. **Advertising is the second act:** $0 (2022) → $1.5B (2025) → ~$3B (2026) → ~$8B (2030, Omdia); first-party data + global scale + light ad load. Source: https://www.thecurrent.com/media/streaming-no-playbook-inside-netflix-quest-double-ad-revenue-3-billion
4. **Live = acquisition + ads, not viewing share:** 6 of top-10 sign-up days in 5 years; ~1% of viewing but >5% of content spend; ~$1B/yr. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/16/netflix-nflx-earnings-q2-2026.html
5. **Local-first global content machine:** majority of new originals non-English; owned studios worldwide. Source: https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/squid-game-korean-content-surge-netflix-non-english-record-1236665198/
6. **Franchise industrial complex:** hits become universes spanning series, films, games, live experiences, consumer products.
7. **Fandom as a business:** Netflix House, Tudum events, sing-along theatricals, creator talent, video podcasts — "a place you go / a culture you join," not just a service you watch.
8. **Competitive self-image:** <10% of TV time = headroom; ~7% of a $670B addressable market captured. Sources: https://deadline.com/2025/01/netflix-q4-2024-earnings-subscribers-nfl-squid-game-1236262728/ ; https://defenseworld.net/2026/07/18/netflix-q2-earnings-call-highlights.html
9. **The 2026 wobble:** stock −43% YoY (Oct 2026), failed WB mega-deal, slowing US growth — the present-day tension a 2046 scenario should inherit. Source: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nflx/market-cap
